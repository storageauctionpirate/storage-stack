Stack meets a Rubik’s cube

HOW TO PLAY

Stack junk like a falling-block puzzle. Rotate to flip every sticker to the next cube color at once, then line up matches. Slide rows and columns like a cube.

  • Junk rolls in through the garage door. Stack it in the unit.
  • Match by COLOR, not by item. A red couch ≠ a blue couch.
  • Fill a whole row with one color = big payout. Same item type too = bonus.
  • 4+ same color in a row side by side also clears for small cash.
  • Rotate (tap / ↑): the shape turns, and every sticker flips to the next cube color at the same time — white › yellow › red › orange › blue › green (then wraps). Use that to pick the color you want to match.
  • Phone: tap = rotate, swipe ←/→ = move, swipe ↓ = drop. Tap the ◀ ▶ arrows beside a row or ▲ ▼ above/below a column to slide it (wraps around).
  • TWIST MODE (hard): each item starts with mixed sticker colors. Rotate still flips every sticker to the next cube color at the same time, and turns the shape. The ↻ FLIP box shows what you'll get. Item pictures stay the same.
  • Slides: you only get 2 row/column slides at a time (the ⇄ SLIDE dots in the bar). They refill when you clear lines. A slide that doesn't clear still spends a charge and speeds the next drop. Slide to set up, then drop, for a combo bonus.
  • Double drop (higher levels, occasional): two pieces fall at once. Tap ⇄ SWITCH, the other piece, or S / Tab to control the other one. Rotate flips colors on both together.
  • Keys: ←/→ move, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space drop, S / Tab switch, P pause.
  • Clear the goal lines to win the unit. Junk reaching the door = game over.
