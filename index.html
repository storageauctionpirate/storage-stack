HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: swipe ←/→. Drop: swipe ↓ or tap DROP.
  • ROTATE·COLOR (tap the unit or the button): the item turns and every part shifts to the next color. Can't turn? The colors still shift.
  • Match by COLOR, not by item: fill a whole row with one color, or line up 4+ of one color. Classic: side by side. Twist: sideways or up and down. Chains pay more.
  • Classic: clear 6 rows to win the unit. Twist: clear 5 rows on level 1, then 6, 7, and 8 after that. Junk reaching the door = game over.
More tips: slides, big finds, doubles, Twist
  • Storage finds roll in through the roll-up door: 4-piece shapes made of totes, boxes and crates: straight 4, L, J, T, S, Z and the 2×2 tote stack or crate stack, plus rarer big finds: hand truck, reefer dolly, refrigerator, washer, dryer, rolled rug, ladder, microwave, cooler, suitcase, toolbox, trunk, and the rarest: L and U sectional sofas.
  • Totes, boxes and crates land as separate items. Big finds stay one object until a clear cuts through them.
  • A full row of one color pays big, doubled if it's all the same kind of item. Classic: 4+ side by side pays small. Twist: 4+ sideways or up and down pays small.
  • A clear cuts straight through anything in that row. What's left of a big item stays as a cut-off piece and drops as one chunk until it lands on something.
  • Color order: blue › yellow › red › green › gray › black (only colors unlocked this level).
  • Slides: tap ◀ ▶ beside any row to slide it (wraps around), or ▼ under a column to cycle it. SLIDE ROW button: tap it, then tap the left/right side of a row. You hold 2 slide charges; clears refill them. A slide that clears nothing still costs a charge and hurries the next drop. Slide to set up, then drop for a combo bonus.
  • Double drop (level 3+, sometimes): two items fall together. Tap ⇄ SWITCH or the other item.
  • TWIST MODE: Match 4 sideways or up and down. Early levels use solid-color finds; later finds may be mixed colors. Rotate still shifts every part one color.
  • Keys: ←/→ move, ↑ rotate·color, ↓ soft drop, Space drop, S/Tab switch, P pause.
