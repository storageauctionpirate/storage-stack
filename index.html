STORAGE STACK

by Storage Auction Pirate

HOW TO PLAY

  • Junk rolls in through the garage door. Stack it in the unit.
  • Match by COLOR, not by item. A red couch ≠ a blue couch.
  • Fill a whole row with one color = big payout. Same item type too = bonus.
  • 4+ same color in a row side by side also clears for small cash.
  • Phone: tap = rotate, swipe ←/→ = move, swipe ↓ = drop. Tap the ◀ ▶ arrows beside a row or ▲ ▼ above/below a column to slide it (wraps around).
  • Keys: ←/→ move, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space drop, P pause.
  • Clear the goal lines to win the unit. Junk reaching the door = game over.
